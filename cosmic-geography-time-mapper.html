How to Read This Map

At Babel, God divided the 70 nations and assigned them to the Sons of God. They rebelled. They became the Princes — the Principalities — over the territories of the earth.

This map traces their stolen empires and Christ's reclamation.

Deuteronomy 32:8–9 — "When the Most High divided the nations, He set their boundaries according to the number of the sons of God. But the LORD's portion is His people."
